### Slide 1: The Problem — The $10.4B AI Personalization Blindspot
- **AI Amnesia**: Despite multi-billion-dollar foundation models, every session with ChatGPT or Claude begins at zero. They don't know who you are, what you read last month, or why your taste matters.
- **Mainstream Degradation**: When high-net-worth users ask for travel or dining guidance, standard LLMs default to commercial tourist traps and generic crowd-pleasers.
- **Lack of Cultural Grounding**: As stated in the Qloo Hackathon theme: *"Agents can reason and plan, but ask them what to recommend, and they are guessing."* LLMs have zero empirical data on why an aesthetic preference in auteur cinema statistically translates into specific natural wine bars and architectural hotels.

---

### Slide 2: The Technology — Hybrid Directed Graph + Live Qloo Intelligence
- **Persistent Directed Taste Graph**: Rather than ephemeral chat logs, TasteHub maintains a temporal, multi-domain graph spanning **6 cultural verticals**: Cinema, Music, Literature, Dining, Architecture/Travel, and Fashion.
- **Empirical Correlation via Qloo**: Directly interfaces with Qloo’s 250M+ entity graph to compute mathematical affinities and over-indexing ratios (e.g. 3.8x above baseline).
- **Transparent 4-Step Agentic Pipeline**:
  1. *Goal Decomposition*: Intent parsing & entity resolution.
  2. *Graph Traversal*: Zero-amnesia memory scan across historical milestones.
  3. *Qloo Tool Execution*: Live API calls against official Hackathon endpoints (`/v2/insights`, `/v2/search`).
  4. *Aesthetic Harmony Synthesis*: Persona vector calculation with 92%+ cohesion.
- **Closed Learning Loop**: Every discovery card includes `+ Add to Graph`, creating a self-refining, compounding data moat.

---

### Slide 3: The Product — TasteHub in Action
- **Interactive Knowledge Graph View**: Temporal scrubber tracking cultural evolution across seasons (Jan 2026 $\rightarrow$ Apr 2026).
- **Conversational Concierge**: Natural language chat rendering live reasoning traces, memory anchors, and verified place cards.
- **City Experience Concierge (City Mode)**: Synthesizes bespoke 4-part day narratives (Morning Coffee Ritual, Afternoon Atelier, Evening Dining, Night Listening Lounge) across Kyoto, Tokyo, London, Paris, Berlin, NYC, and custom global destinations.
- **The Serendipity Engine**: Intentional counter-recommendation generator to break algorithmic filter bubbles through high-uniqueness aesthetic friction.
- **10-Step Interactive Onboarding Tour**: Native SVG spotlight walkthrough allowing judges and enterprise clients to onboard in under 2 minutes.
